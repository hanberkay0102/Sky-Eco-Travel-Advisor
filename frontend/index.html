🌍 How does Sky work?

1

Tell Sky where you want to go

Start by saying something like "I want to travel to Lisbon" or click Plan a Trip. Sky will ask for your destination and origin city.

2

Share your travel details

Sky will ask about your travel dates, budget, number of travellers, and how important sustainability is to you. You can use the calendar picker or type your answers.

3

Get eco-scored transport options

Sky compares trains, buses, cars, and planes for your route — each scored by carbon emissions, price, and travel time. Real data from the Climatiq and OpenRouteService APIs.

4

Explore more

After transport, you can search for eco-friendly accommodation, local activities, weather forecasts, city info, currency rates, and detailed carbon footprint breakdowns.

5

Make greener choices

Every option shows an Eco-Score (0–100). Green means low impact, amber is moderate, red is high. Sky helps you see the environmental trade-offs at a glance.
